## T1. Title screen vista (16:9)

"Authentic 16-bit SNES-era pixel art, a wide cinematic 16:9 landscape painting for a fantasy adventure game's title screen. Crisp pixels, rich but limited palette, soft dithered sky gradients, no anti-aliased blur, no text, no letters, no logo, no UI, no borders. Original designs only (not based on any existing game, character or franchise).

Golden-hour sunrise over a vast magical kingdom, seen from a high grassy cliff.
- **Bottom-right corner:** a small young hero stands on the cliff edge with their back to the viewer, looking out over the land. They wear a green tunic and brown boots, have short red hair, and hold a sword at their side. Their cape stirs in the wind. They take up only about one tenth of the image height.
- **Far distance, upper right:** a dark stone castle with purple-glowing windows sits on a mountain spire under a few swirling storm clouds. It is the villain's castle.
- **Between the cliff and the castle:** a winding river valley with a small village of red-roofed cottages and a wooden bridge, a lush forest, and a sparkling lake with a tiny temple on an island.
- **On the far left:** golden desert dunes with a sandstone ruin.
- **In the sky:** snowy mountains on the horizon, and flocks of birds.
- **The upper third** is mostly a calm, glowing sky with soft clouds and a few early stars fading out.
- **Left third:** keep the middle and lower part calmer and slightly darker (soft forest shadows), because a menu will sit there.
- **Right half, middle:** keep it calm too (open sky and distant hills), because buttons will sit there.
- **Light and mood:** warm sunrise light rakes across the scene, with long shadows. A few glowing fireflies and drifting petals float near the cliff. Hopeful, epic and magical, like the opening of a grand adventure."

Tips:
- If Gemini adds any words or a logo, ask again with "absolutely no text anywhere".
- If the hero comes out big, add "the hero is tiny in the frame, the landscape is the star".
